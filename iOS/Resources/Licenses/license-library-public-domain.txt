PUBLIC DOMAIN LIBRARY EDITIONS

The Library contains 37 editions recorded as confirmed public domain in Angrove’s source manifest. The public-domain designation applies to the bundled text and its historical translation or edition.

Public domain is a rights status rather than an open-source license. These Library texts are not distributed under the software, model, or font licenses listed elsewhere on the Licenses page.

The historical editions are recorded as public domain in the United States. Copyright status can vary by country and by translation or edition; this notice does not apply to every edition of a work with the same title.

The World English Bible was deliberately released into the public domain by its translators and editors. “World English Bible” is a trademark used to identify that translation. Modified text should not be presented as the unmodified World English Bible.

Author attribution appears with each work in the Library section. Modern distributor website material, navigation, and distributor notices are excluded from the reading text. The designation here covers the bundled edition text, not a distributor’s website or additional material.
